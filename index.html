What started with filming his brother skateboarding turned into a full-blown obsession with storytelling. Bailen taught himself photography, cinematography, editing, and directing long before it was ever a career. Bailen comes from outside the traditional commercial pipeline. Raised outside Chicago by his Mexican father and Greek mother, both chefs, he grew up in a home where food, family, and storytelling brought people together. That sense of connection is woven into the way he approaches people and performance.That perspective still shows up in the way he approaches people and performance. He's far more interested in what's happening between people than showing off the camera. After high school, Bailen skipped the traditional path and moved straight to Los Angeles to bet on himself. Since then he's built an impressive body of work across commercials, music videos, and branded content, collaborating with clients including Nike, Adidas, and Lululemon. One of the things that stands out most about Bailen is how naturally he works with talent. His background in acting gives him an instinct for performance, and his films never feel over-directed or overly polished. They're confident, current, and rooted in real people. No matter the scale of the project, Bailen has a way of making the work feel approachable, relatable, and unmistakably human.
